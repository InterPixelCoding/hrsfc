## Notes about the Text
### Thomas
- Lives in Fuessen
	- Small town, c 14000 inhabitants
	- In Bayern, South Germany
	- Munich is the capital
	- Fuessen is almost on the border with Austria
	- The landscape is wonderful - Mountains, lakes etc.
	- The town relies upon tourism
	- Fuessen is a health resort (Kurort)
- Mother - works in hospital
- Father - estate agent (Makler)
- His father rented a holiday house for a visit
- In the summer, his parents have to stick to their work
- Tourists mainly come to see the castle - the dream castle of King Ludwig dem Zweiten, the last Bayersischen king
- Thomas has a summer job at a restaurant near the castle
	- He has to wear traditional Bayern clothing (leather trousers)
- In winter it's much quieter and a bit boring, although one can go skiing nearby
- Thomas likes living in Fuessen, since he has the mountains, it's clean (opposed to a large city)
### Manuela
- Lives in Hamburg
	- Second largest city in germany
	- Also a state, Stadtstaat
	- Has a senate
	- Almost 2 million inhabitants
	- In the north by the Elbe
	- Has a harbour (the largest in Europe)
	- Trade, shipping and business are very important for the city
	- Parks, Avenues, Water ways, Nature reserves
	- Lively art scene and night life
		- Pubs, dance clubs
		- open 24/7
	- Famous pub "Kaiserkeller" had Beatles as guests
	- In the centre, the town is the oldest
	- One can go sailing or go a walk by the shore
- She likes living in Hamburg, she thinks it has everything one needs
